About.

I'm Matthew — most people call me Astro. I'm a 12th grader studying marketing, and somewhere along the way I fell in love with the visual side of it: making ideas look as good as they sound.

I design social posts, banners, and infographics in Canva — my biggest piece so far is a full content campaign for Kitewell AI, an AI front-desk service. I've also been collecting certifications along the way: Anthropic's AI fluency courses, Microsoft and LinkedIn's generative AI program, and HubSpot's marketing track.

No grand plan pinned to the wall yet — just learning things that seem useful, making things while I do, and keeping the door open.
